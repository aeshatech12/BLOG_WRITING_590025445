# Right to Die with Dignity: Euthanasia under Article 21 of the Indian Constitution

**Author:** Aesha Chaudhary  
**Course:** Constitutional Law I  
**Category:** Legal Blog Writing

## About This Blog

This legal blog examines the constitutional development of the **Right to Die with Dignity under Article 21 of the Indian Constitution**. It discusses the distinction between active and passive euthanasia and traces the evolution of Indian jurisprudence through major Supreme Court decisions, including *P. Rathinam v. Union of India*, *Gian Kaur v. State of Punjab*, *Aruna Ramachandra Shanbaug v. Union of India*, *Common Cause v. Union of India*, and *Harish Rana v. Union of India (2026)*.

The blog also examines the constitutional tension between **individual autonomy and the sanctity of life**, the importance of safeguards for vulnerable persons, Advance Medical Directives (living wills), and the need for comprehensive end-of-life legislation in India.

## Key Themes

- Article 21 and human dignity
- Active vs. passive euthanasia
- Right to die vs. right to die with dignity
- Patient autonomy and bodily integrity
- Advance Medical Directives (Living Wills)
- Supreme Court jurisprudence on euthanasia
- Safeguards against coercion and abuse
- Need for comprehensive end-of-life legislation
